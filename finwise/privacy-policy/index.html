FinWise Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2 October 2026

FinWise is a personal finance tracker for Android, developed by Mohammad Ali ("I", "me"). This policy explains what information the app handles, where it is kept, and the choices you have.

In short

  • Everything you enter in FinWise is stored only on your device.
  • FinWise does not connect to the internet, so it cannot send your data anywhere.
  • There is no account or sign-in, and there are no ads, analytics or tracking.
  • I do not collect, sell or share your data. I have no access to it.

1. Information you enter

FinWise only stores information you type in or choose yourself:

  • Financial records: transactions, wallets and balances, budgets, savings goals and contributions, bills, money lent or borrowed and its repayments (including the names of people you enter), net worth entries and categories.
  • Profile details: your name and, if you choose to add them, an email address, phone number and profile photo. These are shown only inside the app. They are never used to contact you and never leave your device.
  • Preferences: currency, theme, language and notification settings.

All of this is saved in a local database inside the app's private storage on your phone. It is not uploaded to any server.

2. Information the app does not collect

FinWise does not collect your location, contacts, SMS messages, bank account credentials, device identifiers, advertising ID, usage statistics or crash reports. The released app does not request internet access, and it contains no third-party advertising, analytics or tracking services.

3. Device permissions

  • Notifications: to show the reminders and alerts you can turn on, such as bill reminders, daily spending summaries, budget alerts, savings goal milestones, weekly and monthly insights, and due dates for money lent or borrowed. These notifications are created on your device; nothing is sent from a server.
  • Alarms & reminders (exact alarms): so reminders arrive at the time you set. If you don't grant it, reminders still arrive, but may be delayed.
  • Run at startup: to restore your scheduled reminders after your phone restarts.
  • Vibration: to vibrate for alerts.

When you choose a profile photo, the app can access only the single image you pick from your gallery. When you import a backup, it reads only the file you select.

4. Reports, exports and backups

You can create PDF or CSV reports and backup files of your data. These files are created on your device and only leave it if you choose to share them using Android's share menu, for example to email, cloud storage or a messaging app. Once shared, the file is handled by the app or service you chose, under that service's own privacy policy.

Backup and export files contain your financial information in readable form, so keep them somewhere you trust.

5. Android device backup

If backup is turned on in your phone's settings, Android may include FinWise's data in your device backup in your Google Account and restore it when you set up a new phone. This is managed by Google and your device settings; I cannot access it. You can turn it off in your phone's backup settings (the exact location varies by device, usually Settings › Google › Backup).

6. Sharing and selling

I do not sell, rent, trade or share your information with anyone, because it never reaches me in the first place.

7. Keeping and deleting your data

Your data stays on your device until you delete it. You can delete individual records inside the app. To erase everything, either uninstall FinWise or go to Settings › Apps › FinWise › Storage › Clear storage.

Because your data is never sent to me, there is no copy on any server to delete, and I cannot recover data that has been lost or deleted. Files you exported and saved elsewhere must be deleted separately.

8. Security

FinWise stores your data in the app's private storage, which Android prevents other apps from reading. To protect it further, use a screen lock on your phone and keep your exported files safe.

9. Children

FinWise is not directed at children under 13, and I do not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

10. Changes to this policy

If the way FinWise handles information changes, for example if an online feature is added, this page will be updated and the effective date above will change. Significant changes will also be mentioned in the app's release notes.

11. Contact
